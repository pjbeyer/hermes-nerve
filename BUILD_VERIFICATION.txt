Known validation boundary
-------------------------
Laya is intended for local live validation on the RTX 4060 lane. OpenJev is wired and structurally tested, but the published 27B serving path requires a substantially larger GPU than the available 8GB RTX 4060. A live OpenJev A/B therefore remains an explicit post-release validation tracked in issue #10 on a >=30GB remote GPU; v0.2.2 must not claim that live OpenJev inference passed until that gate is executed.
